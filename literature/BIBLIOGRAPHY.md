# Literature Bibliography — ReturnGuard

Eight papers supporting the ReturnGuard proposal, one per pipeline/governance component. All links were checked directly against arXiv on 2026-09-17 and are live. PDFs are open-access under arXiv's non-exclusive distribution license, so they may be downloaded and committed to `/literature/` alongside this file.

---

### 1. Multi-agent debate / reasoning pipelines
**Maps to:** Planner → Decision synthesis across specialist agents

Du, Y., Li, S., Torralba, A., Tenenbaum, J. B., & Mordatch, I. (2023). *Improving factuality and reasoning in language models through multiagent debate.* arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325

PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14325
